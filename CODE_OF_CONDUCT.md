# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ynf to be a place where people can contribute without being harassed. We pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take fair and
appropriate action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or
harmful. That includes removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions, and banning contributors temporarily or permanently.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions, and any place where someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@eyelock](https://github.com/eyelock).
Use the repository's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/eyelock/ynf/security/advisories/new) form,
noting that it is a conduct concern, or email support@eyelock.net. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
